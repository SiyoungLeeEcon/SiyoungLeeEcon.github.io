About

Working toward a Ph.D. in Economics

I am an M.A. student in Economics at Sogang University, applying to Ph.D. programs in Economics for Fall 2027. My work sits at the intersection of international finance and spatial economics, and I'm especially interested in how geographic frictions shape cross-country capital flows and exchange-rate dynamics.

Research

Research Interests

My research asks how geography — distance, location, and space — shapes economic outcomes, from capital flows to causal identification.

Theme 01

International Finance & Geography

I study how geographic distance shapes cross-border capital allocation and exchange-rate transmission, using structural portfolio-choice and multi-country exchange-rate models.

Theme 02

Spatial Economics

My work on dynamic spatial equilibrium models examines how demographic outcomes, such as fertility, interact with location choice and local economic conditions.

Theme 03

Econometric Methodology

I develop partial-identification methods for causal inference, including sensitivity analysis for extrapolating treatment effects in regression discontinuity designs.

Working Papers

Working Papers

  1. 2026
    Lee, S.*, Kwak, J. (2026). The Geography of Interest Rate Differences. Working paper. PDF

    Shows that cross-country interest-rate differences rise systematically with geographic distance, and develops a structural portfolio-choice model — estimated on bilateral portfolio holdings and interest-rate data — quantifying how distance frictions and destination characteristics drive this dispersion.

  2. 2026
    Lee, S., Lee, S., Joo, S., Kim, B. (2026). Sensitivity Analysis in Sharp Regression Discontinuity Designs. Working paper. PDF

    Develops a partial-identification framework for extrapolating causal effects beyond the RD cutoff, deriving sharp bounds on average and distributional treatment effects under interpretable, smoothness-based sensitivity measures. Demonstrated via Monte Carlo simulations and an empirical application on external validity.
